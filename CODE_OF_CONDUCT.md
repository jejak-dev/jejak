# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Jejak to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that make this project open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy.

## Our standards

Helpful behavior:

- showing empathy and kindness toward other people,
- being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences,
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully,
- taking responsibility for your mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and
  learning from the experience,
- focusing on what is best for the community as a whole.

Unacceptable behavior:

- sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind,
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks,
- public or private harassment,
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission,
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions) and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers. Use GitHub's private
reporting channel for the repository at <https://github.com/jejak-dev/jejak>
(Security → **Report a vulnerability**), or contact a maintainer directly through
GitHub. Reports are handled as confidentially as the channel allows, and the
maintainers will not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith.

Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith may be temporarily or
permanently excluded from the project.

## Enforcement approach

Maintainers will:

1. **Correct** the offending behavior with a private, clear notice.
2. **Warn** with consequences for continued behavior, including a period of no
   interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporarily ban** from any sort of interaction or public communication with the
   project.
4. **Permanently ban** from any sort of public interaction within the project.

The severity and the response depend on the behavior, its impact, and whether it was
repeated. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled as privately as possible.
